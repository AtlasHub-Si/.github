# AtlasHub — Open-source and intellectual-property policy

**Baseline: 2026-11-25. Scope: AtlasHub-Si repositories.**

AtlasHub prefers Apache License 2.0 for new, independently authored software intended for open distribution, after ownership, dependency and asset clearance. MIT remains valid for MIT-derived projects. This preference is not a blanket relicensing of existing work.

## Mandatory rules

1. Preserve original copyright, license texts, NOTICE files, attribution and applicable source obligations.
2. Do not label upstream code as Atlas-owned. Identify Atlas-authored additions and their actual copyright owner.
3. Do not convert AGPL, source-available, proprietary or restricted components to Apache/MIT by editing a root LICENSE. Review commercial distribution and network-use obligations separately.
4. A public repository without an explicit verified license is not automatically open source. Resolve ownership and all license locations before granting permissions.
5. Brand assets, trademarks, third-party images, fonts, models, binaries, services and provider terms require separate review; a software license does not grant trademark rights.
6. No official binary, SaaS release or commercial distribution before a pinned-source inventory, dependency/SBOM review, notices, provider terms, security tests and release approval.
7. Corporate entities are named institutionally as AtlasGlobal Tech LTD and AtlasHub Brasil; copyright ownership must be documented per contribution and must not be inferred from branding or geography.

## Current foundation decisions

- AtlasBot: OpenMausBot Apache-2.0 candidate; retain upstream license and notices.
- AtlasHQ: Paperclip MIT; retain MIT notices.
- AtlasWorkforce: agency-agents MIT; retain attribution.
- AtlasDesk: DeskcommCRM MIT; retain attribution.
- AtlasClub: LearnHouse AGPL-3.0 foundation; no blanket relicensing. Review enterprise-only modules separately.
- Legacy OpenBot-derived work: freeze public distribution pending provenance and rights audit; an Atlas-authored MIT notice alone is not proof of upstream redistribution rights.
- New Atlas-authored code: Apache-2.0 is preferred where a documented rights review permits it.

This policy is not itself a license grant. Each repository's verified applicable licenses and notices control. If a license is absent or inconsistent, stop distribution until resolved.
